BORING

Officially the least exciting website on the internet. Nothing to buy, nothing to book, and nothing ever happens. Move your head and the screen turns into a window. That is the entire feature list. Keep your expectations low.

About

BORING is one hallway where nothing happens. The page redraws it from the position of your eyes, so the screen behaves like a window instead of a picture.

Turn on head tracking and your webcam finds your eyes about 30 times a second. The video is analysed inside this browser tab and never leaves your computer.
